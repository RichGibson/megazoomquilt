About

I was part of the GigaPan project from 2008-2012. It was an amazing period of creative engagement for me.

For the past 14 years I have made periodic efforts to display my images in a system that I control. In May, with Claude Code, and obra's superpowers skill, I finally achieved that goal, and now most of the panoramic images that I have created are here on https://megazoomquilt.com.

Most of the images on Megazoomquilt were shot with GigaPan robot mounts. But I also experimented with doing hand held multi image capture, and high resolution macro and micro images.

The code is open source, on GitHub at github.com/RichGibson/megazoomquilt, but it contains a lot of things that are relevant to my particular use cases. I'd be happy to collaborate if you have a related use case.

You can tile any image and make it zoomable, so, for example, creating an archive of conference posters that is easy to navigate would be a great use case!

In the greatest compliment or burn I could imagine, my son in law said it is "exactly what I would think that you would design."
